OUR MISSION

What RideQuetta Aims to Improve

As an early-stage startup, we are focused on solving the genuine daily transit challenges faced by Quetta commuters and drivers.

Problem 01

Clearer fare expectations

Eliminating arbitrary overcharging before starting a journey through clear, distance-based transparent fare estimates calculated specifically for Quetta routes.

Problem 02

Easier local ride requests

Saving passengers from walking long distances to main roads or waiting indefinitely in harsh winter or summer weather just to hail a passing rickshaw.

Problem 03

Dignified driver opportunities

Providing hardworking Quetta drivers with an organized booking channel that reduces fuel wasted on empty rounds and guarantees fair compensation.
